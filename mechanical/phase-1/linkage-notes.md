# Linkage Notes

Start with conservative linkage geometry and travel. The motor reverses at the YL-99 endpoints, so the linkage must move through the complete bounded range without crossing an over-center lock, binding, or relying on continuous crank rotation.

Mount the wiper motor on the stationary base plate, off-center from the lazy susan bearing. Attach the linkage to a rear corner bracket or tab on the rotating top plate unless testing shows a better leverage point. The linkage does not need to attach at the rotation center.

Watch for:

- Binding near each travel end.
- Linkage collision with the bearing or top plate.
- Safety flag missing the YL-99 roller.
- Linkage reaching a dead-center or over-center condition before either switch actuates.
- Mechanical hard stop contact before the corresponding switch actuates.
- Motor mount flex under load.
- BTS7960 heat sink enclosed without airflow.
